# Supported games

Every game with a save works with Save Editor Paradise: you can always **back it up, restore it** and use **[Find a value](README.md#find-a-value)**. The games below also have a **built-in editor**, marked with an **Editor** badge in the app.

← [Back to the README](README.md)

## Zelda

| Game | Title ID | What you can edit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild** | `01007EF00011E000` | Rupees, hearts, stamina, Mons, Korok seeds, materials, food, arrows, key items; weapons, bows and shields with **unbreakable** (∞) per item or for all, copy and delete, free-slot counts and bonuses shown; **[Shrine Tracker](README.md#shrine-tracker-zelda)** for all 136 shrines |
| **The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom** | `0100F2C0115B6000` | Rupees, hearts, stamina, battery, materials, food, Zonai devices, key items; weapons, bows and shields with **unbreakable** (∞), copy and delete, bonuses and food effects shown; **[Shrine Tracker](README.md#shrine-tracker-zelda)** for all 152 shrines |
| **Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity** | `01002B00111A2000` | Rupees, 160+ materials and the level of every character |

Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom can also show the game's [real item icons](README.md#real-game-icons-zelda).

## Any other game

| What you get | How |
| --- | --- |
| Backups and restore | The **Backups** button on the game's screen |
| Edit numbers such as money, HP or lives | **[Find a value](README.md#find-a-value)** turns a number you can see in the game into a field you can edit |

## Want another game?

[Request it](https://github.com/eduardo-santos/switch-save-editor-paradise-releases/issues/new?template=game_request.yml). It helps a lot to say what you'd like to edit and whether a save editor for it already exists on PC or the web.

Some games can't get an editor: their save format isn't known publicly, or it's encrypted. Online-competitive games (such as Splatoon or Mario Maker) are left out on purpose, because edited saves there are a ban risk.
